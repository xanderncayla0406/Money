# Lunch call script: junk removal owners

Goal for one lunch: **15 dials, 2–3 real conversations, 1 "yes, set me up."**
Most calls won't be answered. That's normal. Text the ones who don't pick up (template at the bottom).

---

## 1. Who to call (make the list before lunch, ~10 min)

- Google Maps: search **"junk removal"** in your city plus 2–3 nearby towns.
- **Call:** small local companies. Owner answers the phone, 1–3 trucks, 10–200 reviews.
- **Skip franchises:** 1-800-GOT-JUNK, College Hunks, Junk King, JDog, LoadUp. Head office picks their software.
- **Best targets:** a website or Facebook page that says **"text us a photo for a quote."** They already have the exact problem this fixes.

---

## 2. The call (2–3 minutes)

**Open**
> "Hey, is this the owner? I'm [name], I'm local, I'll be quick. Do customers text you photos to get a price?"

Let them talk. Then:
> "How long does it take you to answer those? Do you ever lose some because you're on a job or it's late at night?"

**Pitch**
> "I built a link you put on your website, Google listing and Facebook. The customer snaps a photo of their junk, it estimates how much of your truck it'll take, and gives them a price from **your** price sheet right then. They pick a day, and you get the photos, the price and their phone number. It works at 10pm while you're asleep. Final price is still confirmed on site, same as now."

**Offer**
> "I'm setting up a few local companies free for 2 weeks. I'll set it up with your prices; you don't have to do anything. After that it's $49 a month, cancel anytime. One quarter-load job pays for it for months. Want me to set you up?"

## 4. Ask everyone these, even the ones who say no

This is the real research. Write the answers down.

1. How do you quote now: photos, phone, or show up in person?
2. Roughly how many quote requests a week? How many turn into jobs?
3. What do you use for scheduling: Jobber, Housecall Pro, paper, nothing?
4. What would make $49 a month a no-brainer? What would make it a no?

---

## 5. Objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "AI can't price junk." | "It gives a range and says final price on site, same as you do now from photos. You see every photo before you go." |
| "I already use Jobber / Housecall Pro." | "Keep it. This just turns photos into booking requests. You put them in Jobber like any other job." |
| "Customers just call me." | "The ones who don't call are the ones you're missing. People browsing at 10pm don't call." |
| "I don't want tire-kickers." | "You get their name and number before they see a price, so you can call them back." |
| "Too expensive." | "One quarter-load job covers 4+ months. And the first 2 weeks are free." |
| "Send me some info." | "Sure, what's your email? I'll send a link you can try with a photo right now." |

**Don't promise** it will book a certain number of jobs, and don't say other companies already use it. It's new; say so if asked. "You'd be one of the first, which is why it's free to try."
