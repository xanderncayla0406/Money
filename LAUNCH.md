# Launch plan: Etsy spreadsheet shop on a $150 budget

## The decision, and why

| | Dropshipping | **Digital spreadsheets on Etsy (chosen)** |
|---|---|---|
| Startup cost | Store ~$39/mo plus ads. Guides say $500–$2,000 to start, and $300–$500 *per product* just to test ads | About $0.20 per listing, plus a one-time setup fee of about $15 if Etsy charges it |
| Margin | 5–20% net after ads; most beginners break even or lose money for 60–120 days | About 82–87% of each sale (Etsy takes 6.5% + 3% + $0.25 + $0.20 renewal) |
| Traffic | 100% paid. No ads, no sales | Etsy has its own buyers searching for "budget spreadsheet" |
| Risk | Supplier delays, refunds, chargebacks, inventory problems | No inventory, no shipping, delivery is automatic |
| Does $150 work? | **No.** It isn't enough for one proper product test | **Yes**, with about $100 left for ads |

Timing helps too. Christmas planners sell from mid-November to mid-December, and budget planners have their big moment in late December and January (New Year's resolutions). That's why there are two products: one for right now and one for the New Year.

**Honest expectation:** a brand-new Etsy shop often gets **zero sales in its first 1–3 weeks** while Etsy's search learns about it. This is a "first sales in days or weeks, then it grows" plan, not "money today." Each sale costs nothing to fulfill, so every one is almost pure profit, and the files keep selling without more work.

## What YOU do (about 60–90 minutes, today)

Etsy needs a real person's name, bank account and ID. That part has to be you.

1. **Open the files yourself first** (5 min). Import one into Google Sheets, type a purchase and watch it update. Etsy requires a human to review AI-assisted products, and you should know what you're selling.
2. **Create the shop** at etsy.com/sell (20 min). Pick a shop name (e.g. something like "ClearBudgetCo"). Add your bank account and a card. Etsy shows any setup fee before you confirm.
3. **Create the 3 listings** (30 min). Copy everything from `listings/etsy-listings.md`, upload the `.xlsx` files as the digital files and the images in numbered order. Prices: **$4.99 / $8.99 / $11.99**.
4. **Shop basics** (10 min). Add a shop icon (crop `images/budget-planner/01-cover.jpg` to a square) and a one-line shop announcement such as "Simple spreadsheets for budgets & holidays. Excel + Google Sheets."
5. **Turn on Etsy Ads** (see the budget below).
6. **Free traffic: Pinterest** (15 min, then 5 min a day). Make a free Pinterest business account and pin each listing image linked to its Etsy listing. Budget and Christmas planners are some of the most-searched topics on Pinterest. Pin 3–5 a day.
7. **Tell people you know.** Post the Christmas tracker on your Facebook, Instagram or TikTok and in family group chats. Your first 1–3 sales and reviews matter more than anything else for Etsy ranking.

## How to spend the $150

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Etsy shop setup fee (only if Etsy shows one) | ~$15 | Sources disagree; Etsy shows the exact amount at signup |
| 3 listings × $0.20 | $0.60 | Each renews for $0.20 after a sale or after 4 months |
| **Etsy Ads, weeks 1–2** | $3/day = **$42** | Turn on for all 3 listings |
| **Etsy Ads, weeks 3–4** | up to $3/day = **$42** | Only on listings that got clicks or sales; turn off the rest |
| Reserve | ~$50 | Don't spend it until something is clearly selling, then put it into ads for that listing in late Dec / early Jan, when budget-planner demand peaks |

Rules for ads: check Etsy Stats every 3–4 days. If a listing gets 100+ ad clicks and no sales, the problem is the image, title or price, not the ad budget. Tell me and I'll make new images and titles.

**Profit per sale after Etsy fees** (6.5% transaction + 3% + $0.25 processing + $0.20 renewal): $4.99 → about $4.07 · $8.99 → about $7.69 · $11.99 → about $10.40.
Break-even on the full $150 is about 15–37 sales, depending on the mix.

## Week-by-week

- **Week 1 (now):** go live, Etsy Ads on, pin daily, share with people you know. Aim for your first sale and first review.
- **Weeks 2–4 (to mid-December):** the Christmas tracker sells. Answer every message within a few hours (it affects your ranking).
- **Late Dec to January:** push the 2027 Budget Planner hard. This is its peak season. Move your reserve into ads for it.
- **After that:** add more listings using the same build scripts. Ideas: a debt payoff tracker sold on its own, a wedding budget, a small business income & expense tracker, a paycheck budget. More listings means more search coverage. Ask me and I'll build them.
