Every field below is checked against Etsy's limits: titles are at most 140 characters, there are exactly 13 tags, and each tag is at most 20 characters.

**Settings for all three listings**
- Listing type: **Digital files**
- Who made it: **I did** · What is it: **A finished product** · When was it made: the most recent range (e.g. **2020 – 2026**)
- Category: *Paper & Party Supplies → Paper → Calendars & Planners* (the planner) or *… → Templates* (if Etsy suggests it)
- Renewal: **Automatic**
- Quantity: 999
- Upload the images from `images/<product>/` in numbered order. Image 01 is the thumbnail.
